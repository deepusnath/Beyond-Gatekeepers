# The 90-Day Pilot: Track A, Space Evolve instrumented from birth

*Approved under Operating Thesis D6 (25 July 2026). This folder holds the pilot's design and, as sessions happen, its evidence.*

## What we are testing

Can a chapter launched under the ratified playbook prove, with light-touch measurement from session one, that the Evolve model creates autonomy, competence, and relatedness, and hold its rhythm while doing so?

## The design

- **Chapter:** Space Evolve, first Saturday monthly, FAYA Trivandrum, launching September 2026
- **Duration:** first 3 sessions (Sep, Oct, Nov 2026)
- **Instruments:**
  1. Session logs per the [template](../templates/session-log-template.md), within a week, every session
  2. The [pulse survey](../templates/pulse-survey.md) at sessions 1 and 3
  3. The six-move pattern check: which moves from the [Kerala playbook](../genesis/kerala-community-playbook.md) did the chapter execute this month?
- **What we deliberately do not measure:** attendance growth targets, social media reach, anything that would push the chapter toward spectacle.

## Success and failure, defined in advance

- **Success:** three consecutive logged sessions (rhythm held), pulse completed twice, next-session date announced in the room every time, and at least one contributor (scribe, speaker, or volunteer) who was not on the founding team.
- **Worth reporting as failure:** a missed month, a pulse we skipped because it felt awkward, relatedness trailing badly, zero emergent contributors. Failures go in the report with the same font size as successes.

## The report

Day 90 (early December 2026): one honest document in this folder, reviewed by one independent reader who is not FAYA staff and not a chapter lead. It ends with its own decision log: continue, adjust, or stop.
