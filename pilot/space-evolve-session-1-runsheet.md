# Space Evolve, Session 1: The Run Sheet

**Saturday, 5 September 2026 · FAYA, Trivandrum · Lead: Anurag Reghu**
*The first chapter launched fully inside the network's machinery, and Track A of the 90-day pilot: instrumented from birth. This sheet turns the [host-a-session playbook](../playbooks/host-a-session.md) into one page for one day.*

## Before the day (owner: chapter lead + FAYA ops)

- [ ] Venue confirmed at FAYA; projector, seating that can face inward for open floor, chai
- [ ] First speaker confirmed (a practitioner who has built; the qualifying question: "what have you built or broken with this?")
- [ ] Announcement posted where spacetech people actually are (IIST and CET circles, μLearn channels, LinkedIn); personally invite 5 people who'd add to the room
- [ ] Scribe named for session 1 (rotate after; it's a credit, not a chore)
- [ ] **Pulse slips printed** ([print sheet](./pulse-slips.html), 8 per A4); pens
- [ ] **October's date decided** (first Saturday: 3 October), ready to announce from the stage

## Run of show (90 minutes)

| Time | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Doors, mingling | Never skip; latecomers need a soft edge |
| 0:15 | Welcome (2 min) | What Evolve is, the FAYA:80 lineage in one breath, and: **"next session is October 3, same place"** |
| 0:20 | Main talk (30–40 min) | Practitioner, real work, demos beat slides |
| 1:00 | Open floor | Protected naive-question time; first question to a student or first-timer if a hand is up |
| 1:25 | **Pulse** (60 seconds) | Hand out slips, collect in a box; anonymous, no discussion needed |
| 1:30 | "Formally done" | The real session: hangout, chai, introductions; do not rush the room empty |

## The three sentences the MC must say

1. "This chapter is part of the Evolve network, the continuation of twelve years of FAYA:80."
2. "The naive question is protected here; seniority buys no priority."
3. "Next session: Saturday, October 3, right here."
