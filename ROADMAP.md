## Phase 1 — Prove the rhythm (months 1–3)

- [ ] Every Forming chapter holds its first logged session with the next date announced in the room
- [ ] First chapter reaches 🟢 Active (3 consecutive logged months)
- [ ] `good first issue` pipeline live: scribe roles, charter proposals, playbook improvements
- [ ] Announce the repo to μLearn's 48k learners — recruit scribes and apprentice leads from the community

## Phase 2 — Open the network (months 4–6)

- [ ] First community-proposed chapter chartered (a domain or city not on the original list)
- [ ] Apprentice leads named in every Active chapter (month-6 charter requirement)
- [ ] Cross-chapter speaker exchange: each Active chapter hosts one speaker from a sibling domain
- [ ] Playbook v2: fold real learnings from Phase 1 retros back into the playbooks

## Phase 3 — Compound (months 7–12)

- [ ] 3+ chapters Active simultaneously; first chapter outside Kerala chartered
- [ ] First leadership term renewals conducted in the room, per governance
- [ ] Evolve ↔ ecosystem loops live: session topics feeding [Beyond-Borders](https://github.com/The-Purple-Movement/Beyond-Borders) use-cases; [Beyond-Syllabus](https://github.com/The-Purple-Movement/Beyond-Syllabus) study groups forming at chapter sessions
- [ ] Annual network retro: what FAYA:80's 12-year lens says about our first 12 months

## What we will not do

- No ticketed events. No "pro" tiers. No sponsor-curated agendas.
- No growth targets for attendance. Rhythm and leader-development are the metrics.
- No new chapter approvals faster than leaders emerge. Empty charters help no one.
