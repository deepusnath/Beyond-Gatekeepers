# Playbook: Launch an Evolve Chapter

FAYA:80 ran for 144 consecutive months. This playbook compresses those twelve years into the shortest path to a chapter that lasts. Nothing here is theory.

## Before you start — the honest checklist

- [ ] You can personally commit to **the same day, every month, for 12 months**. Not "most months." Every month. This is the whole game.
- [ ] You have **a co-lead** (charters require two — no solo heroes, no single points of failure).
- [ ] You've attended at least 3 sessions of any Evolve (or were a FAYA:80 regular) so you know the feel of the room.
- [ ] Your domain has at least ~15 people within reach who'd show up. Small is fine — FAYA:80's magic was never headcount.

## Step 1 — Charter (week 1)

1. Fork this repo, copy `templates/CHARTER-template.md` to `chapters/<your-chapter>/CHARTER.md`.
2. Fill everything. The two decisions that matter most:
   - **The slot.** Pick a day you can defend forever (e.g. "second Thursday"). Check it doesn't collide with existing chapters in your city.
   - **The venue.** A fixed, free, easy-to-reach room beats a fancy rotating one. Colleges, company spaces, and co-works all work — as long as the host understands they're a *venue*, not a *gatekeeper*.
3. Open a PR. Existing chapter leads review within a week.

## Step 2 — First session (weeks 2–5)

- **Format for session one:** one practitioner talk (30–40 min) + open floor (30 min) + hangout. Do not overprogram. The gaps are the product.
- **First speaker:** someone who *builds* in the domain, not someone who *markets* in it. Your credibility is set by your first three speakers.
- **Announce the next session's date at the first session.** Always. The rhythm is announced before it's asked for.
- Log it in `sessions/` within a week.

## Step 3 — Reaching Active status (months 2–3)

Three consecutive logged sessions = 🟢 Active. Until then you are 🟡 Forming, and that's fine. What kills forming chapters, in order:

1. **Skipping a month** ("attendance was low, let's regroup"). Never. Run it for 6 people. FAYA:80 ran through a pandemic.
2. **Upgrading to spectacle.** Resist the big-event temptation. Monthly and modest beats quarterly and grand.
3. **Lead burnout.** This is why you have a co-lead. Split MC duty from month one.

## Step 4 — Compounding (months 4–12)

- **Name an apprentice lead by month 6** (charter requirement). Your success metric is the chapter running without you.
- **Feed the network:** unusual formats that worked, speaker contacts, venue tips → PR them back into this playbook.
- **Cross-pollinate:** invite a speaker from an adjacent Evolve once or twice a year. The unlikely-room effect is where collaborations start.

## The rules you cannot bend

Fixed rhythm. Free entry. Sponsors support, never steer. No stage monopoly. Log every session. (Full text: [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md))

## What the network gives you

- The Evolve name and Purple Movement identity, with 12 years of earned credibility behind it
- Playbooks, templates, and a leads' channel of people who've solved your problem already
- Speaker pipeline via μLearn (48k+ learners), sibling chapters, and the movement's industry network
- A permanent public record of your chapter's work — session logs live in Git, forever
