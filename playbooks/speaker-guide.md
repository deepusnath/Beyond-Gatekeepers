# Speaker Guide

Thank you for speaking at an Evolve. You're joining a lineage of 224 speakers across twelve years of FAYA:80 — and the same house rules that made those rooms special apply to yours.

## What makes an Evolve talk land

- **Speak from the scar tissue.** The room is here for what you built, broke, shipped, and regretted — not a product overview. The best FAYA:80 talks were war stories with diagrams.
- **Assume smart, assume mixed.** Your room has students next to CTOs. Don't dumb it down; do define your acronyms the first time.
- **30–40 minutes, then open floor.** The Q&A is not an epilogue here — it's half the point. Leave real time and expect real questions, including beautifully naive ones. Treat those with the most respect; that's the culture.
- **Demos beat slides.** A terminal, a prototype, a failed experiment — anything real.

## House rules

- **No sales pitches.** Mentioning your company/product in context: fine. A talk that exists to sell: not fine, and the MC will steer you off it.
- **Free means free.** Don't gate your slides or follow-ups behind sign-ups.
- **Your credit is permanent.** Your talk gets a session log in this public repo — name, topic, links to your slides/repo. Send the scribe your links within a week.

## Logistics

- Arrive 20 minutes early; mingle — the pre-session room is where your best questions get seeded.
- Formats welcome: standard talk, live build, postmortem, "everything I know in 30 minutes", panel-of-one AMA.
- Stay for the hangout after if you can. Historically, that's where the collaborations started.
