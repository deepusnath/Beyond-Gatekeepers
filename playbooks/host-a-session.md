# Playbook: Host a Monthly Session

The 90 minutes are the easy part. This is the full month, compressed.

## T-3 weeks

- Confirm speaker + topic. One main talk is enough. Ask the speaker the FAYA:80 qualifying question: *"What have you built or broken with this?"* Practitioners only.
- Send the speaker the [Speaker Guide](./speaker-guide.md).

## T-2 weeks

- Announce: date, venue, speaker, topic. Post where your people actually are (college groups, μLearn channels, LinkedIn, the chapter channel).
- Confirm the venue and the basics: projector, chairs that can face each other for open floor, chai/snacks if you can.

## T-1 week

- Reminder post. Personally invite 5 people who'd add to the room — a student who asks good questions, a senior who tells honest war stories.
- Line up your scribe (session logger). Rotate this; it's a contributor role, not a chore.

## Session day

**Run of show (90 min default):**

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Doors + informal mingling (never skip; latecomers need a soft edge) |
| 0:15 | Welcome — 2 min on what Evolve is, for first-timers. Announce **next month's date**. |
| 0:20 | Main talk (30–40 min) |
| 1:00 | Open floor — protected naive-question time. MC's job: pull in the quiet ones, gently park the monologuers. |
| 1:30 | "Formally done" — then the real session: hangout, chai, introductions. Do not rush the room empty. |

**MC rules of thumb:**
- First question after the talk goes to a student or first-timer if one has a hand up.
- Nobody — sponsor, lead, or visiting dignitary — gets unplanned stage time. (Governance rule; it protects you from awkward conversations. "It's network policy" is your shield.)
- End by naming next month's date out loud. The rhythm is spoken, every time.

## T+1 week

- **Log the session** in `sessions/` from the [template](../templates/session-log-template.md). No log = it didn't happen.
- Thank the speaker publicly with a link to their session log — that's their permanent credit.
- 15-minute co-lead retro: what worked, what to change, who might speak next.
