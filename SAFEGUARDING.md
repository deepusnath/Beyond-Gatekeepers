# Safeguarding Policy (draft for council ratification)

> **Status:** draft prepared for the first network council. The campus pilot does not begin until this policy is ratified and two safeguarding contacts are appointed. That sequencing is binding (Operating Thesis D6).

## Why this document exists

The movement works with young people, including minors on campuses. A movement that promises belonging owes its participants safety as a precondition, not a feature.

## The rules

1. **Two named safeguarding contacts** exist at all times at network level, publicly listed, reachable by any participant directly and confidentially. Every chapter names its own local contact at charter time.
2. **Open settings.** Movement activities happen in public or institutional spaces. No private one-to-one meetings between adults and minors under the movement's banner; mentorship conversations with minors happen in open view or with another adult present.
3. **Consent for minors.** Campus activities involving participants under 18 require institutional consent (the college) and follow the institution's parental-consent norms.
4. **Data minimization.** Surveys and pulse instruments are anonymous by default. No personal data of minors is collected beyond attendance counts. Photos at events follow venue and institutional policy, with an easy opt-out.
5. **Incident path.** Any participant can report a concern to a safeguarding contact, the chapter lead, or through the code-of-conduct process. Reports are acknowledged within 48 hours. The person reported against never handles the report.
6. **Consequences are real.** Safeguarding violations lead to removal from leadership roles and, where warranted, from the community and referral to institutional or legal authorities. No role, seniority, or contribution history creates exemption.
7. **Training.** Safeguarding contacts complete a recognized basic safeguarding orientation before appointment. Chapter leads receive a one-page briefing at charter time.

## What this is not

This policy does not make the movement a guardian, counselor, or investigator of matters beyond its events and platforms. It makes one promise: within movement spaces, someone responsible is always reachable, and reports never disappear.

## Review

Reviewed by the network council annually, and immediately after any incident.
