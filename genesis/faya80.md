# Genesis: FAYA:80 (2013–2025)

*Every Evolve chapter inherits its DNA from here. This is the record.*

## The numbers

- **June 2013 → June 2025**: twelve years of monthly sessions, **no month ever missed** — including the COVID-19 lockdowns
- **200+ sessions · 224 speakers · 20,000+ attendees**
- Recognized as **Kerala's longest-running technology meetup**

## What it was

**FAYA:80** (read: *FAYA port 80*) was a monthly technology session hosted by FAYA at Technopark, Thiruvananthapuram — every **first Wednesday** of the month, at the **"Floor of Madness"**, FAYA's space where anyone could walk in and pitch their wildest, most unpolished idea without fear.

It began in June 2013 from a simple conviction: technology professionals in Kerala should never lag behind on what's next. It grew into something bigger — a *ritual*. Developers, founders, students, marketers, and the just-plain-curious showed up month after month, not for certificates, but to belong.

Milestones:

- **July 2017** — 50th edition celebrated with **Disrupt Kerala 2017**, a day-long event with global speakers, launching two sibling chapters: **Kochi** (second Wednesday) and **Kozhikode** (third Wednesday)
- **2020–2021** — the sessions continued through lockdown without breaking rhythm
- **June 2025** — the grand finale at Technopark. Not an ending: a deliberate handover.

## What made it work (the DNA every Evolve inherits)

1. **Rhythm over spectacle.** "It wasn't about big moments. It was about showing up every month, no matter what." Kerala's tech scene never lacked bold launches; it lacked continuity. FAYA:80's radical act was consistency.
2. **Belonging over broadcasting.** The best parts happened around the sessions — chai, late-night momos, conversations that ran to early morning. Sessions were designed to spill over.
3. **Cross-pollination.** Engineers heard marketing heads on gamification; founders heard product leads demystify AI. Unlikely rooms produced unlikely collaborations.
4. **The protected naive question.** Students sat with CTOs in unhurried conversation. Every question found space. μLearn — now 48,000+ learners — found its fertile ground exactly here, using FAYA:80 as its living classroom.
5. **Real-world anchoring.** From cloud to blockchain to AI to scaling war stories — talks were given by people living the technology, not summarizing it.

## Why it ended — and why that's the point

In June 2025, FAYA:80 closed its centralized Technopark chapter **on purpose**. Twelve years proved the model; the next step was to stop being one room and become many. "Beyond FAYA:80" set out to decentralise the culture into domain-focused, peer-led micro-communities across cities and campuses — lighter formats, same DNA.

Those micro-communities are the **Evolve network** this repository governs: AI Evolve, HR Evolve, Quantum Evolve, IoT Evolve, Space Evolve, and every chapter yet to be chartered.

> "Kerala doesn't suffer from a lack of talent; it suffers from a lack of continuity. FAYA:80 was our answer to that."
> — Deepu S Nath, founder
