# The Kerala Model — a Community-Building Playbook

*Distilled from Deepu S Nath's working document "History of Community Building in Kerala" (2026), compiled to help Evolve chapters — and anyone, anywhere — adopt the model. The original opens with the thesis every chapter lead should memorize:*

> **"The deepest problem was never a shortage of talent. It was a shortage of confidence, connection, and permission."**

## The theory stack (why it works)

The model isn't folklore — it's built on named frameworks, deliberately:

1. **Community of Practice (CoP)** — domain + community + practice. Every Evolve chapter is a CoP; the domain focus is what makes a room compound knowledge instead of just networking.
2. **Self-Determination Theory** (Deci & Ryan — the same foundation μLearn cites publicly):
   - **Autonomy** — members choose projects, mediums, languages; leaders emerge, they're not appointed
   - **Competence** — challenges, bootcamps, Top-100-style talent filters; visible skill progression
   - **Relatedness** — learning circles, mentorship, the unhurried hangout after every session
3. **The Karma economy** — proof-of-work points earned through contribution, validated by **peer + mentor approval**, unlocking further opportunity. HR Evolve extended this into hiring: Karma as a unified skill currency. *(This is the "contribution is the currency" rule in our GOVERNANCE, formalized.)*
4. **Learning-circle architecture** (μLearn's three layers — the template for chapter sub-groups): Think-Tank meetings (knowledge curation + proof of work), Office Hours (plug into the network on demand), and Learning Circles (small peer groups, the fundamental building block).
5. **Supporting models**: the Museum Model (curated, self-paced exposure), Austin's Butterfly (public, kind, iterative critique), and the Quadruple Helix (government + industry + academia + community at one table — visible in every milestone above that has an MOU or an IAS officer in the room).

## The repeating pattern (the actual playbook)

Across 2013 → 2025, the same six moves repeat:

1. **Start a free, fixed rhythm** in a domain that matters (Coffee@DBG, HR Evolve, every future chapter)
2. **Name the roles** — enabler jobs with owners, not committees with vibes
3. **Make contribution the currency** — proof-of-work, peer-validated, publicly credited
4. **Sign the legitimacy** — institutions join paper-first (NASSCOM, MOUs, Technopark venues)
5. **Run talent filters as celebrations** — Top 100s, In50Hrs, challenge events that find people rhythm alone won't surface
6. **Decentralize at peak** — hand the thing over while it's strong, to people the rhythm itself developed

Chapter leads: when you're unsure what to do next, find where your chapter is in this list and do the next move.
