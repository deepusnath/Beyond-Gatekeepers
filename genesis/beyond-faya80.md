## Why this document matters

The finale wasn't a goodbye, it was a governance act: the community watched one chapter graduate (HR Evolve), saw the model named (Beyond Gatekeepers), and joined the movement that carries it (Purple). Every future chapter's graduation ceremony inherits from this day.
