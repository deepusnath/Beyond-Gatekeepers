# Beyond Gatekeepers — the Evolve Network

> **Knowledge is not held. It is shared.**

**Beyond Gatekeepers** is the community-of-practice pillar of [The Purple Movement](https://purple-movement.com/) — a network of monthly, recurring, peer-led meetups called **Evolves**, where practitioners and learners in a domain gather in person, share what actually works, and grow leaders instead of audiences.

This repository is the open, permanent record of that network: its charters, playbooks, session logs, and governance. If you want to attend an Evolve, start one, or speak at one — everything you need is here.

---

## The lineage: 12 years of proof

Evolve is not an experiment. It is the deliberate succession of **[FAYA:80](./genesis/faya80.md)** — Kerala's longest-running technology meetup:

- **200+ sessions · 224 speakers · 20,000+ attendees**
- **Every month from June 2013 to June 2025** — including through the COVID lockdowns, without missing a single month
- Kochi and Kozhikode chapters, the "Floor of Madness", and a culture where a student's naive question got the same room as a CTO's war story

In June 2025, FAYA:80 closed its Technopark chapter on purpose — not because it failed, but to **decentralise the magic**. One centralized series became a network of domain-focused chapters. That network is Evolve.

Read the full genesis story: [genesis/faya80.md](./genesis/faya80.md)

## What is an Evolve?

An **Evolve** is a domain-focused community of practice with three non-negotiables:

1. **A fixed monthly rhythm.** Each chapter picks a recurring slot (e.g. "first Wednesday, every month") and never misses it. Consistency is the product — this is the single deepest lesson of FAYA:80's 12 years.
2. **An emergent leader, not an appointed one.** Chapters form when a community leader steps up, picks a location, and commits to the rhythm. Leadership is earned by showing up.
3. **Free and open, forever.** No tickets, no gatekeeping, no pay-to-speak. Sponsors may support; they never steer.

## How this repository works

```
Beyond-Gatekeepers/
├── genesis/            # The FAYA:80 record — where this culture comes from
├── chapters/           # One folder per Evolve chapter
│   └── <chapter>/
│       ├── CHARTER.md  # Who leads, where, when, and the chapter's focus
│       └── sessions/   # One markdown log per session — the proof of rhythm
├── playbooks/          # How to launch a chapter, host a session, brief a speaker
├── templates/          # Charter + session-log templates
└── GOVERNANCE.md       # How leaders emerge, how we defend the culture
```

**The session log is the heartbeat.** Every session gets a markdown file — date, speakers, topics, attendance, learnings. That's how a chapter proves its rhythm, how speakers get permanent public credit, and how the network compounds knowledge instead of losing it. FAYA:80's biggest near-loss was its history living on one website; here, the record is in Git, forkable and forever.
