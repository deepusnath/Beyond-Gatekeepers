# Governance — how Evolve stays Evolve

This document exists because we know exactly how community platforms die. We watched it happen around us for twelve years while FAYA:80 kept going. The failure modes are not hypothetical; they are documented history in Kerala's ecosystem, and this governance model is built as a direct defense against each one.

## The three named threats

1. **Platform capture.** A community becomes someone's personal promotion stage or another entity's influence channel. Defense: rotation, sponsorship rules, and the no-stage-monopoly norm below.
2. **Complacency.** The rhythm slips — "we'll skip this month" — and the spark dies. Defense: the rhythm rule is constitutional; a chapter that misses two consecutive months without force majeure reverts to Forming status.
3. **Idea drain without credit.** Others scale what the community starts, and contributors see their work vanish. Defense: everything in this repo is MIT-licensed *with attribution preserved in Git history* — forking is encouraged, erasure is impossible.

## Chapter leadership

- **Leaders emerge; they are not appointed.** Anyone who has attended 3+ sessions of any Evolve (or was a FAYA:80 regular) may propose a chapter or volunteer to co-lead an existing one.
- **Every chapter has at least two co-leads.** No single point of failure, no single point of ego.
- **Leads serve renewable 12-month terms**, marked in the chapter charter. Renewal is by simple consent of the chapter's active participants at a session — visibly, in the room.
- **Succession is a first-class duty.** A lead's success is measured by whether the chapter runs without them. Each charter names an apprentice lead by month 6.

## The session rules (all chapters)

1. **Same day, every month.** The slot is chosen at charter time and changes only with a charter amendment.
2. **Free entry, always.** No tickets, no paid tiers, no "premium" access.
3. **No stage monopoly.** No person may speak (as main speaker) more than twice in any rolling 6-month window — including the chapter leads and including anyone from FAYA or the Purple Movement core.
4. **Sponsors support, never steer.** Sponsors may cover venue, food, or travel. They may be thanked. They may not pick speakers, set topics, gate attendance, or collect attendee data. A sponsor slide is fine; a sponsor keynote is not.
5. **Every session is logged.** A session without a log in `chapters/<name>/sessions/` didn't happen, as far as the network is concerned. The log is the chapter's proof of rhythm and the speakers' permanent credit.
6. **The naive question is protected.** Sessions reserve open floor time. Seniority buys no priority in the queue. This is Floor-of-Madness DNA.

## Chapter lifecycle — the incubation model

Evolve chapters are not franchises; they are **incubated communities**. FAYA acts as the incubator: it hosts, mentors, and carries the operational load for a chapter's first months — then the chapter **graduates** into a self-governing community with its own executive council and team. Graduation is the goal from day one; a chapter that cannot outgrow its incubator hasn't finished forming.

| Status | Meaning | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| ⚪ Proposed | Charter PR open | Charter using the template, lead(s) identified |
| 🟡 Forming | Charter merged, launch scheduled | First session scheduled with fixed monthly slot |
| 🟢 Incubating | Rhythm proven, FAYA-hosted | 3 consecutive monthly sessions logged; FAYA provides venue, mentoring, and operational support for the initial **3–6 months** |
| 🟣 Graduated | Self-governing community | Own **executive council and team** formed and named in the charter; runs its own venue, funding, and program while remaining part of the Evolve network and bound by the constitutional rules |
| 🔴 Dormant | Rhythm broken | 2 consecutive missed months → reverts to Forming; 6 months dark → archived (never deleted — the record stays) |

**Graduation checklist** (amended into the chapter's charter at graduation):
- Executive council elected/confirmed in the room, with named roles and term dates
- Independent venue and funding arrangement (self-funded/non-profit, per the free-entry rule)
- 6+ consecutive logged sessions
- Apprentice-lead pipeline in place

The first graduate of this model is **HR Evolve** — launched at Evolve India 2023 (FAYA:80's 100th edition) under Deepa Nair's leadership, venue funded by FAYA for the first year, then offered free by Technopark as the community proved itself, now running as a self-funded non-profit with its own identity at [hrevolve.org](https://hrevolve.org/), while remaining part of the network. That arc — incubator funds → institution adopts → community self-sustains — is the graduation path every chapter should expect.

## Network-level decisions

Changes to this governance file, the templates, or the playbooks happen by pull request, discussed in the open. Chapter leads collectively review; The Purple Movement core team holds merge rights as stewards, not owners — their explicit mandate is to *protect the rules above from erosion, including erosion by themselves*.

## Amendment

Anything in this document can change **except the three session rules that define us: fixed rhythm, free entry, no capture.** Those are the constitution. Everything else is policy.
