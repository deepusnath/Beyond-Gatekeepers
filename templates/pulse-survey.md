# The Pulse: three questions, twice per quarter

*The movement's lightest measurement instrument, grounded in Self-Determination Theory. Anonymous, paper or form, under 60 seconds. Used at instrumented chapters (pilot chapters use it at sessions 1 and 3; established chapters quarterly).*

## The three questions

Rate each from 1 (not at all) to 5 (completely true):

1. **"I choose what I engage with here; nobody's script is running me."** *(autonomy)*
2. **"I can point to something I understand or can do better since I started coming."** *(competence; at a chapter's first session, use: "I expect to learn things here I couldn't learn alone")*
3. **"Someone in this room knows my name and what I'm working on."** *(relatedness)*

Plus one open line, optional:

> **"What question are you taking home tonight?"**

## Rules of use

- **Anonymous, always.** No names, no IDs, no demographic fields beyond an optional first-timer checkbox.
- **Report medians and counts, not stories.** The scribe adds one line to the session log: `Pulse (n=23): A 4 / C 4 / R 3, first-timers 6`.
- **Low relatedness is the alarm that matters most.** A room can score high on learning and still be failing as a community. If R trails A and C by more than a point for two consecutive measurements, the chapter lead's next month is about introductions, not speakers.
- **Never gamify the pulse.** No targets, no chapter leaderboards, no praise for high scores. The moment scores become goals, the instrument dies. (This is the pack's own warning about gamification undermining intrinsic motivation, applied to ourselves.)
- The open-line answers are the qualitative gold. Scribes may quote them in session logs, unattributed.

## Language

English and Malayalam versions side by side on the same slip. Translation must be validated by two native speakers before first use (evidence-discipline requirement; not yet done, tracked in the pilot plan).
